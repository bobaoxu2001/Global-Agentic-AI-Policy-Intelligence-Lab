# AI Policy Atlas

**Global Agentic AI Policy Intelligence Lab** — an independent portfolio project that turns policy change into business implications, product controls, public-affairs work products, and explicit decision gates.

> **Readiness split:** the applied policy research portfolio is ready for public review; the structured production corpus remains publication-gated. AI-assisted preview research is not legal advice and has not completed independent human legal review.

## Project quick read

The project demonstrates six parts of an applied global AI policy research workflow:

1. **Monitor:** a [July-August policy-change refresh](docs/deliverables/POLICY_REFRESH_2026-08-06.md), [dated global policy intelligence brief](docs/deliverables/GLOBAL_AI_POLICY_INTELLIGENCE_BRIEF_2026-08-06.md), and [agentic AI / semiconductor / compute / cloud radar](docs/deliverables/EMERGING_TECH_POLICY_RADAR.md).
2. **Synthesize:** an [executive briefing deck](docs/deliverables/Global_AI_Policy_Executive_Briefing.pptx), [decision brief](docs/deliverables/EXECUTIVE_DECISION_BRIEF.md), and [policy memo](docs/deliverables/POLICY_MEMO_HUMAN_OVERSIGHT_AND_DISCLOSURE.md).
3. **Translate:** a [business implications matrix](docs/deliverables/BUSINESS_IMPLICATIONS_MATRIX.md) and [Tencent public-information implications brief](docs/deliverables/TENCENT_PUBLIC_INFORMATION_IMPLICATIONS_BRIEF.md) connecting policy signals to business, product, cloud, security, and engineering questions.
4. **Operationalize:** an [applied AI risk-management method](https://ai-policy-atlas-ax2183-5057-ao-xus-projects.vercel.app/risk-management) that separates NIST foundation, Atlas proposal, and product decision question across GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE.
5. **Engage:** a [policy-position sample](docs/deliverables/POLICY_POSITION_NOTE_SAMPLE.md), [consultation response](docs/deliverables/CONSULTATION_RESPONSE_SAMPLE.md), [speaking brief](docs/deliverables/SPEAKING_BRIEF_SAMPLE.md), [industry initiatives tracker](docs/deliverables/INDUSTRY_INITIATIVES_TRACKER.md), [stakeholder landscape](docs/deliverables/STAKEHOLDER_LANDSCAPE.json), and [fictional meeting brief](docs/deliverables/MEETING_BRIEF_SAMPLE.md).
6. **Calibrate:** source tiers, lifecycle status, confidence, fact → inference → recommendation chains, an [ADRS sensitivity note](docs/deliverables/ADRS_SENSITIVITY_NOTE.md), and [generated corpus analysis](docs/deliverables/POLICY_CORPUS_DESCRIPTIVE_ANALYSIS.md) with explicit denominators.

The repository keeps the evidence and honest limits explicit. The dated policy refresh, current validation results, and exact Preview deployment record provide the public review baseline.

The Preview interface supports **English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, and Portuguese** from the global language selector. The choice persists across navigation and reloads. Interface translation is a reading aid: official titles, quotations, source IDs, legal numbers, dated evidence, and downloadable artifacts remain in their verified language.

## The operating decision

The portfolio does not claim that one configuration is lawful everywhere. Its current executive posture is narrower and more useful:

> Build reusable identity, authorization, logging, disclosure, approval, testing, and redress controls now. Hold any universal launch decision until product facts, current primary law, operating evidence, and qualified market-specific review are available.

This is the throughline from policy source to accountable action:

`policy change → scoped fact → analytical implication → product/control owner → evidence gate → decision`

## Research product and evidence model

The Atlas classifies policy instruments by type, bindingness, lifecycle, and regulatory approach; maps relevant provisions to agentic capabilities and governance risks; and maps those to technical and organizational controls.

`ADRS = min(100, InherentRisk × (1 − MitigationCredit) × J)`

ADRS supports prioritization and discussion. It is **not** a legal determination, compliance certification, probability estimate, or empirically calibrated risk score. See the [methodology decisions](docs/METHODOLOGY_DECISIONS.md) and [sensitivity note](docs/deliverables/ADRS_SENSITIVITY_NOTE.md).
