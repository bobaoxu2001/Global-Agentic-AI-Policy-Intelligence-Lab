## Material risks and opportunities

- **Risk:** pending SB 1000 text silently replaces current California law.
- **Risk:** a voluntary code, framework, research publication, or cooperation initiative is presented as regulation.
- **Risk:** destination alone is used for advanced-compute screening.
- **Risk:** a control is claimed without evidence that it works in the real workflow.
- **Opportunity:** identity, least privilege, logs, approval, disclosure, provenance, testing, vulnerability response, and redress become shared platform capabilities.
- **Opportunity:** dated assurance evidence supports standards, sandbox, government, and enterprise engagement without forcing one legal conclusion.

## Five launch gates

1. System and capability profile confirmed.
2. Dated primary sources checked against exact product facts; current law and pending proposals separated.
3. Controls tested in the real workflow, not merely planned.
4. Sensitivity, unresolved questions, and re-verification triggers shown.
5. Qualified human review and accountable decision recorded.
