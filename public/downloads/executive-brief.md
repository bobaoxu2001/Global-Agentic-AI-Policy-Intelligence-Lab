# Executive Decision Brief — Global Agentic AI Launch Readiness

**As of:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** final independent portfolio work sample; underlying structured policy records remain `in_review`
**Decision owner:** hypothetical global AI leadership team
**Boundary:** AI-assisted research; not legal advice, a company recommendation, or launch authorization

## Decision

**Do not issue a single global launch approval based on this preview alone. Proceed with reusable control development and require product-specific, market-specific review before deployment.**

## Why this is the defensible call

| Market | Source-backed signal | Evidence | Business implication (analysis) | Decision now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **European Union** | Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 is in force; Article 50 transparency duties have applied since 2026-08-02. Its targeted transition to 2026-12-02 applies only to Article 50(2) marking by qualifying systems placed on the market before 2026-08-02; it did not postpone the other Article 50 duties. GDPR Article 22 already applies to covered solely automated consequential decisions. | S01, S02, S13, G8-EU-GDPR22 | Disclosure/provenance is a current workstream, distinct from deferred high-risk conformity. Consequential workflows still need fact-specific analysis. | Record the Article 50 paragraph, role, surface, control, exception analysis, and operating evidence; keep unreviewed consequential decisions outside a solely automated path. |
| **China** | A binding anthropomorphic-interaction rule took effect on 2026-07-15 for scoped continuous emotional interaction; a separate May agent implementation opinion is policy direction. Existing GenAI and labeling rules remain separately classified. | S15, S16, G8-CN-LABEL, G8-CN-GENAI | Consumer/social companion services need a targeted scope screen; ordinary assistants should not be swept in without the continuous-interaction trigger. | Separate binding duties from policy direction; validate scope, disclosure, safety, privacy, minors, dependency, exit, assessment, filing, labels, and metadata against authoritative Chinese text. |
| **United States** | California's core covered-provider AI Transparency Act provisions became operative on 2026-08-02 inside a fragmented federal, state, sector, consumer, privacy, and export-control screen. | S04, S05, S06, S14, G8-US-CO-SB26-189, G8-US-EO-14179 | Provider scale, modality, platform role, customer ownership, end use, state, and sector can change the analysis. | Classify California role/threshold/modality and run the broader product/state/sector/export screens; build documentation and control evidence now. |
| **Singapore** | The 2026 agentic-AI framework and sandbox work provide non-binding governance and testing direction. | S08, S09 | Assurance evidence can reduce adoption friction without being treated as legal authorization. | Crosswalk controls and nominate a low-risk, evidence-ready sandbox use case. |

## Material risks and opportunities

- **Risk:** a high-risk delay is misread as a general transparency delay.
- **Risk:** the shared EU/California date is mistaken for shared scope.
- **Risk:** China's scoped anthropomorphic-interaction rule is generalized to every chatbot.
- **Risk:** sparse coverage or a low heuristic score is treated as evidence that no duty applies.
- **Risk:** permanent authority, supplier, customer, or regional commitments close policy options too early.
- **Opportunity:** identity, least privilege, logs, approval, disclosure, testing, and redress become shared platform capabilities.
- **Opportunity:** assurance and standards participation can turn implementation evidence into trust and interoperability advantages.

## 30-day operating plan

1. **Policy — Day 5:** refresh the applicability screen; record status, bindingness, dates, source IDs, confidence, and the product fact creating the question.
2. **Product + Legal — Day 10:** classify each market configuration across direct interaction, generated content, consequential decisions, profiling, minors, sectors, and distribution.
3. **Engineering + Security — Day 21:** implement and test expiring authority, allowlisted tools, logs, approval thresholds, kill switch, disclosure, and redress.
4. **Leadership — Day 30:** review evidence, sensitivity, challenged assumptions, unresolved questions, owners, and re-verification triggers before any market decision.

## Five launch gates

1. System and capability profile confirmed.
2. Current primary sources checked against the exact product facts.
3. Controls tested in the real workflow, not merely planned.
4. Sensitivity and unresolved questions shown.
5. Qualified human review and accountable decision recorded.
